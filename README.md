# COLD CASE NDE-08 — The Victorian census that launched psychical research cannot carry its own number

Family: apparitions  |  Disposition: **POSTABLE_AS_COLD_CASE**  |  claim_authority=NONE
Members: sidgwick-crisis-arithmetic-v1, phantasms_boundary_repair_v1, phantasms_four_clock_documentation_lag_v1

## The case
In 1894 the Sidgwick Census of Hallucinations computed that crisis apparitions - seeing someone at the hour of their distant death - occurred 440 times too often for chance. That arithmetic founded a field. The lab re-ran it and audited the Phantasms case books behind it.

## How it died
The numerator came from voluntary appeals and personal networks while the exposure denominator was guessed, so the famous ratio answers a counterfactual question no one asked. The documentation-lag audits died at census scale: only 3 identifiable long-lag families against a floor of 10 - the Victorian case books do not carry enough datable long-delay cases to test whether stories sharpened over time.

## What still stands
The arithmetic reproduction (the computation itself checks out given its inputs) and a complete 63-case clock census of Phantasms. Ceiling: historical bookkeeping.

## What would revive it
A same-funnel census: any historical or modern collection with FIXED eligibility, every eligible non-event counted, and report timing recorded before outcome news - diary studies, dated letter archives, even institutional logs.

## Card fields (G18 contract)
- plain_meaning: The founding statistic of apparition research divides a carefully collected numerator by an invented denominator. The stories may still be interesting; the famous number is not.
- what_it_is_not: Not proof crisis apparitions are chance, because a broken denominator breaks both directions; and not a slight on the Victorian collectors, because their case work was meticulous - it is the population arithmetic that fails.
- next_decisive_test: A registered prospective collection where the at-risk population is defined before any story arrives.
- what_we_need: Dated diary or correspondence archives recording experiences BEFORE news arrived, or any modern registry design partner willing to run the fixed-funnel version.
